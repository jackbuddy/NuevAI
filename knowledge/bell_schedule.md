# Upper School bell schedule (confirmed by the school)

## Daily schedule: blocks, order and times
Each Upper School student has 4 class blocks a day (unless there is special programming). Blocks 1-4 meet on A and A' days. Blocks 5-8 meet on B and B' days.

Standard times:
- Blocks 1 and 5: 8:55-10:10 AM
- Blocks 2 and 6: 10:15-11:30 AM
- Lunch: starts at 11:30 AM and ends at 12:00 or 12:30 PM, depending on whether advisory (or another activity) takes the second half of lunch
- Blocks 3 and 7: 12:35-1:50 PM
- Blocks 4 and 8: 1:55-3:10 PM

## A, A', B and B' days: what the prime means
A' is the same as an A day but the last two blocks are flipped, and B' is the same as a B day with its last two blocks flipped. This trades off which class loses time when students leave early for games, so it is not always the same class.
- A day order: Block 1, Block 2, Block 3, Block 4
- A' day order: Block 1, Block 2, Block 4, Block 3
- B day order: Block 5, Block 6, Block 7, Block 8
- B' day order: Block 5, Block 6, Block 8, Block 7

So on an A' day Block 4 meets at 12:35-1:50 and Block 3 meets at 1:55-3:10. On a B' day Block 8 meets at 12:35-1:50 and Block 7 meets at 1:55-3:10.
Special-schedule days (for example Community meetings, assemblies or department meetings) can change the regular times.

## Special bell schedules on Community-time days and full-day events (Upper School, 2026-27)
Source: The Sheet - Master Schedule 2026-27, "Special Schedule Configurations" on the Calendar AB + Special Schedule tab (Upper School, school year 2026-27). The master calendar marks each affected date "Community (40')", "Community (90')" or "Full-Day Event"; on those dates the day's own record states the times. The block numbers below are the day's first to fourth class in its own order (on a B day they are Blocks 5-8, and on A' and B' days the last two swap as usual).
- Community (40') days, 65-minute classes: first class 8:55-10:00 AM; second class 10:05-11:10 AM; assembly or activity 11:20 AM-12:00 PM; lunch 12:00-12:50 PM; third class 12:55-2:00 PM; fourth class 2:05-3:10 PM.
- Community (90') days, 55-minute classes: first class 8:55-9:50 AM; second class 9:55-10:50 AM; Community time 11:00 AM-12:30 PM; lunch 12:30-1:10 PM; third class 1:15-2:10 PM; fourth class 2:15-3:10 PM.
- Full-day events (no classes): morning activities 8:55-11:30 AM; lunch 11:30 AM-12:30 PM; afternoon activities 12:30-3:10 PM.
School still ends at 3:10 PM on all of these days. Dates with another label (Advisory, Dept Meetings, Division Meeting, All School Meeting) use the standard times unless the day's record says otherwise.

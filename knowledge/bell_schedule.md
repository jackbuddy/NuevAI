# Upper School bell schedule (confirmed by the school)

## Daily schedule: blocks, order and times
Each Upper School student has 4 class blocks a day (unless there is special programming). Blocks 1-4 meet on A and A' days. Blocks 5-8 meet on B and B' days.

Standard times:
- Blocks 1 and 5: 8:55-10:10 AM
- Blocks 2 and 6: 10:15-11:30 AM
- Lunch: starts at 11:30 AM and ends at 12:00 or 12:30 PM, depending on whether advisory (or another activity) takes the second half of lunch
- Blocks 3 and 7: 12:35-1:50 PM
- Blocks 4 and 8: 1:55-3:10 PM

## A, A', B and B' days: what the prime means
A' is the same as an A day but the last two blocks are flipped, and B' is the same as a B day with its last two blocks flipped. This trades off which class loses time when students leave early for games, so it is not always the same class.
- A day order: Block 1, Block 2, Block 3, Block 4
- A' day order: Block 1, Block 2, Block 4, Block 3
- B day order: Block 5, Block 6, Block 7, Block 8
- B' day order: Block 5, Block 6, Block 8, Block 7

So on an A' day Block 4 meets at 12:35-1:50 and Block 3 meets at 1:55-3:10. On a B' day Block 8 meets at 12:35-1:50 and Block 7 meets at 1:55-3:10.
Special-schedule days (for example Community meetings, assemblies or department meetings) can change the regular times.

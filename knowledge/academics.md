# Upper School academics: graduation requirements and course load (2026-27 Course Catalog)

Last updated: 2026-11-11

## Upper School graduation requirements 2026-27: how many years of each subject (English, history, math, science, languages, arts, PE, SEL, Quest)
Source: "Nueva Graduation Requirements" table in the 2026-27 Course Catalog (Google Doc, captured 2026-10-22). Applies to: the 2026-27 school year. Departments' course lists are in the separate department directory records.
- Arts: 1 year: either 2 semesters of any two Performing Arts electives, or 2 semesters of Visual Arts (Intro and Advanced in the same medium).
- English: 4 years: English 9, 10 and 11 (full year each) plus an English 12 elective in the fall and one in the spring.
- History: 3 years: History 9, 10 and 11 (full year each).
- Math: 3 years; courses vary by student because placement is by "stage not age".
- Science: 3 years: Chemistry (9th grade) and Biology (10th grade), plus a third year of science (two semester electives or one year-long elective), taken concurrently or in 11th or 12th grade.
- World Languages: 3 years, 3 consecutive years of the same language (placement is "stage not age"; Chinese, Japanese and Spanish).
- SEL / DCI: 4 years: SEL 9 + DCI (Design Create Innovate) 9; SEL 10; SEL 11; Senior Block (fall) and SEL 12: The Good Life (spring).
- Physical Education: 4 years (every year of enrollment). Quest: 4 years (every year of enrollment).

## Upper School course load by grade level, 2026-27: core courses and number of electives
Source: "Course Load by Grade Level" in the 2026-27 Course Catalog (captured 2026-10-22). Applies to: 2026-27.
- 9th grade: English 9, SEL 9 + Design Create Innovate (DCI), History 9, Math, a World Language, Chemistry and an arts course, plus electives; up to 4 electives a year (2 per semester).
- 10th grade: English 10, SEL 10, History 10, Math, a World Language, Biology, plus electives; up to 4 electives a year.
- 11th grade: English 11, SEL 11, History 11, Math, a World Language, a science elective, plus electives; up to 6 electives a year (3 per semester).
- 12th grade: English 12 (two electives, fall and spring), Senior Block (fall) and SEL 12 (spring), plus electives; up to 12 electives a year (6 per semester).
- Students are enrolled automatically in their core courses and use the Course Preference Form to choose electives each semester. Each student has 8 course slots (Blocks 1-8: Blocks 1-4 on A and A' days, Blocks 5-8 on B and B' days).

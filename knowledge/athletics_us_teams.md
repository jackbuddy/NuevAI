# Upper School athletics 2026-27: teams, levels, leagues, practice times, schedules and sources

Last updated: 2026-11-12

Verified on 2026-11-12 against the school's own sources first (athletics page, Upper School Athletics Handbook, Student & Family Handbook, US P.E. page, public athletics calendar, team schedule sheets, school emails); MaxPreps and other third-party sites are only supporting evidence and never override them. Confidence: high = two or more official sources agree and games are on an official schedule; medium = official lists agree but no schedule is posted yet; low = a single source or sources conflict.

## Which Upper School sports have girls, boys, coed, varsity or JV teams in 2026-27? (summary)
Source: athletics page "Sports Offered" (read 2026-11-12), US P.E. page, public athletics calendar and the team schedule sheets. Applies to: 2026-27 school year.
- Girls only: Volleyball (fall; varsity and JV), Tennis (fall; varsity and JV), Golf (fall), Beach Volleyball (spring).
- Boys only: Volleyball (spring).
- Girls and boys teams: Cross-Country (fall; the boys also race a JV team), Basketball (winter; boys varsity and JV, girls varsity), Soccer (winter; varsity), Swimming & Diving (spring), Track & Field (spring). The handbooks call swimming and track "Coed".
- Tennis (boys, spring) and Golf (spring: boys' on the PE page, coed on the athletics page): see the conflicts record.
- JV teams that have games on a schedule: Girls' Volleyball, Girls' Tennis, Boys' Basketball and boys' cross-country (JV race). No JV schedule exists for girls' basketball (its sheet says "Varsity Only" for one game), soccer or golf; MaxPreps lists JV pages for girls' soccer, boys' tennis and boys' volleyball that no school source supports.

## When do Upper School seasons start and end? (season dates, tryouts, parent meetings)
Source: US P.E. requirement page (seasons), athletics calendar, team schedule sheets, school emails. Applies to: 2026-27 school year.
- Seasons: Fall is September to November, Winter is November to February, Spring is February to May (US P.E. page). Fall sports start earlier: girls' volleyball's first practice was August 7, 2026 and its first contests August 27.
- Fall Sports Athlete/Parent Meeting: Aug 24, 2026 (7:00 PM). Winter Sports Athlete/Parent Meeting: Nov 4, 2026 (7:00 PM, moved from Nov 3). Spring Sports Athlete/Parent Meeting: Feb 1, 2027 (7:00 PM).
- Winter: basketball's first practice is Nov 2, 2026, scrimmages start Nov 16 and the first contests Nov 23 (24 games maximum); boys' basketball held tryouts in the first week (school email of Oct 30, 2026). Playoffs: PSAL playoffs Feb 13-16, CCS Feb 17-27, 2027 (basketball), NorCal Mar 2-9 and CIF State Mar 12-13 (boys' basketball sheet).
- Cross-country: PSAL championships Nov 5, CIF-CCS Championships Nov 14, CIF State Championships Nov 28, 2026. Girls' tennis ended with CCS playoffs Nov 9-18, 2026. Girls' volleyball finished with CCS Nov 7, 2026.
- Spring sports have no schedule posted yet (the athletics calendar stops at the end of February 2027).

## When and where do Upper School basketball teams practice? (shared gym grid, Nov 2026)
Source: the "Practice Schedule" tabs of the US Boys Basketball and US Girls Basketball Google Sheets (read 2026-11-12), Nueva US Gym, San Mateo. Applies to: the 2026-27 winter season. Coaches change the grid by email; a coach email overrides this.
- The US Gym is split into a front half and a rock-wall half (full gym on Fridays and Saturdays).
- Monday and Wednesday: Boys Varsity 3:30-5:00 PM; Boys JV 5:00-6:30 PM (5:00-5:30 on the full court); Girls Varsity about 5:30-7:00 PM.
- Tuesday and Thursday: Girls Varsity 3:30-5:00 PM; Boys JV 5:00-6:30 PM; Boys Varsity 5:30-7:30 PM.
- Friday (full gym): Girls Varsity 3:30-5:00 PM; Boys JV 5:00-6:00 PM; Boys Varsity 6:00-7:30 PM.
- Saturday (full gym): Boys Varsity and JV 8:00-11:00 AM; Girls Varsity 11:00 AM-12:30 PM (the boys' sheet says 11 AM-2 PM and 5:30-7:30 PM on Mondays for girls: the two sheets differ slightly, the girls' sheet is used for girls).
- Girls' basketball also lists a fall preseason of open gym and lifting sessions after school on Tuesdays and Fridays from Sept 8; official practice begins Nov 2.
- Other sports' practice times are not published (volleyball practices in the US Gym; venues are in the team records).

## Where can I find Upper School game schedules, and which teams have none posted yet?
Source: the public athletics calendar (nuevaschool.org/school-life/athletics/athletics-calendar), the team schedule sheets (Boys Basketball varsity and JV, Girls Basketball, Girls Volleyball varsity and JV) and MaxPreps (third-party). Read 2026-11-12. Applies to: 2026-27 school year.
- Official US schedules: the athletics calendar page covers every Upper School team through Feb 27, 2027 (fall: girls' tennis, golf, volleyball, cross-country; winter: basketball, soccer). It lists each team and level, opponent, home or away and time, but no venue and no results.
- Team schedule sheets add venues (for volleyball), notes (canceled, moved) and the JV volleyball and basketball schedules. When a sheet and the calendar page disagree on a time or a date, the team sheet is used and the disagreement is noted on the game.
- Third-party MaxPreps schedules fill in only games and results the school's own sources lack, and are labelled third-party.
- No schedule yet: all spring teams (Boys' Volleyball, Boys' Tennis, Golf, Swimming & Diving, Track & Field, Beach Volleyball). Schedules usually appear on the calendar shortly before each season; ask athletics@nuevaschool.org or the coach for earlier dates.
- Not published anywhere found: scores for most games (a few are in school emails), coach names for most teams, tryout dates other than the parent meetings.

## Is MaxPreps right about Nueva's teams? (what MaxPreps lists that Nueva does not have)
Source: MaxPreps school page for The Nueva School (San Mateo), third-party, read 2026-11-12, compared with the school's own lists. Applies to: 2026-27 school year.
- MaxPreps is community-edited and partly stale: use the school's own pages first.

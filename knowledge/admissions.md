Last updated: 2026-11-11

## Tuition 2026-27 by grade (Pre-K through Grade 12) and trip costs
Source: "Affording a Nueva Education" page, https://www.nuevaschool.org/admissions/affording-nueva, read 2026-11-11 ("Tuition Rates 2026-27"). Applies to: the 2026-27 school year. A 2027-28 tuition table is not published on that page yet.
- Pre-kindergarten: tuition and fees $48,785 (no trips fee), total $48,785.
- Kindergarten: $48,785 + trips $40 = $48,825.
- 1st grade: $48,785 + trips $100 = $48,885.
- 2nd-4th grade: $48,785 + trips $500 = $49,285.
- 5th-8th grade (Middle School): $65,065 + trips $3,200 = $68,265.
- 9th-12th grade (Upper School): $65,065 + trips $4,400 = $69,465.
So Lower School tuition is $48,785, Middle and Upper School tuition is $65,065 (2026-27).

## Financial assistance at Nueva: how much help, who qualifies, key dates
Source: "Affording a Nueva Education" page, read 2026-11-11. Applies to: families applying for the 2027-28 school year (the page labels the cycle 2026-27).
- Nueva meets 100% of a family's demonstrated financial need with grants, not loans. About $9 million in need-based assistance supports 20% of students.
- Households earning under $150,000 a year (with assets typical of that income) receive a 100% grant; transportation, lunch, trips, enrichment and after-school services are also provided at no cost. Households earning $150,000 to $250,000 contribute 1-10% of income toward tuition. Households earning over $250,000 with demonstrated need also receive assistance.
- Key dates: Financial Assistance Workshop Monday, December 14, 2026 (register through Ravenna). Admissions application due Tuesday, January 5, 2027 (Ravenna). Financial assistance application and all documents due Wednesday, February 3, 2027 (Clarity platform). Admissions and financial assistance decisions released Friday, March 12, 2027 at 4 p.m. Enrollment contracts and deposits due Friday, March 19, 2027.
- Spanish-language help: Alex Lazo, Financial Assistance and Family Partnership Coordinator, alazo@nuevaschool.org, 650-235-7110.

## How to apply to Nueva: application fee, deadline and steps (entry fall 2027), all grades
Source: Visit and Apply pages and the Admissions FAQ on nuevaschool.org, read 2026-11-11. Applies to: applicants for fall 2027 (the 2027-28 school year).
- Application fee: $100, nonrefundable, paid with the Family Information Form in Ravenna (the online admissions system). Fee waivers are available for families applying for financial assistance (details in Ravenna, or email alazo@nuevaschool.org).
- Deadline: all application materials are due January 5, 2027. Guarantee Deadline: Tuesday, November 3, 2026 (Kindergarten and grades 5-10 only): applicants who have the prerequisites by then are guaranteed an in-person Activity Session.
- Steps for Upper School (grades 9-12): create a Ravenna account; Family Information Form and $100 fee; Parent/Guardian Written Questionnaire; Parent/Guardian and Student video questionnaire (Kira); Activity Session on campus (hands-on activities, a math exercise and a timed writing sample); Student Questionnaire and Nueva Creative Project; recommendations (principal or counselor, English teacher, math teacher) and transcripts sent by the current school.
- Steps for Middle School (grades 5-8): Ravenna account; Family Information Form and $100 fee; Wechsler IQ assessment with in-range results; video questionnaire; Activity Session; recommendations and transcripts from the current school.
- Upper School does not require or accept entrance tests: Nueva does not require or accept ISEE or SSAT scores, and an IQ assessment is not required for grades 9-12 (recent results may be emailed to admissions@nuevaschool.org). IQ assessment is required for PreK through Grade 8 (it must be in Nueva's target range).

- Main entry points: pre-kindergarten, kindergarten, and grades 5, 6 and 9; applications are welcome for grades PreK-10 as openings occur; grades 11 and 12 only in special circumstances. - Events: Program Previews (September-November), student-led San Mateo campus tours for Upper School (weekdays October-December, after the Family Information Form), Middle School campus tours (September-December), and virtual office hours (September-December). Register for all events in Ravenna.

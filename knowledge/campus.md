# Campuses and contact (from the school website's contact page)

## Campus locations and main phone numbers
The Nueva School has two campuses.
- San Mateo Campus (the Upper School, grades 9-12): 131 E 28th Avenue, San Mateo, CA 94403. Front Desk phone 650-235-7100.
- Hillsborough Campus (Pre-K through Middle School): 6565 Skyline Boulevard, Hillsborough, CA 94010. Main Office phone 650-350-4600.

## Which campus is the Upper School on
The Upper School (grades 9-12) is on the San Mateo Campus at 131 E 28th Avenue, San Mateo, CA 94403. For general questions call the San Mateo Front Desk at 650-235-7100.

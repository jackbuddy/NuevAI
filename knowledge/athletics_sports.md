## Which Upper School sports are listed in only some sources (golf, ultimate frisbee, flag football, swimming, track): conflicts and how they are resolved
As of 2026-11-12, comparing the school's lists for the Upper School. Applies to: 2026-27.
- Core sports that every current list agrees on: Girls' Volleyball, Cross-Country, Girls' Tennis, Boys' and Girls' Basketball, Boys' and Girls' Soccer, Boys' Volleyball, Boys' Tennis, Swimming & Diving, Track & Field, Girls' Beach Volleyball, and golf.
- Golf (RESOLVED in part): Girls' Golf in the FALL is real: the athletics calendar lists ten Girls' Golf matches from Sept 1 to Nov 3, 2026 (ending with "US Girls Golf @ CCS Team & Individual Championships") and the PE page lists Girls' Golf (Fall); the athletics page and the athletics handbook venue table do not show a fall golf team, so those lists are incomplete. Spring golf exists, but it is "Boys' Golf" on the PE page and in the athletics handbook venue table and "Coed Golf" on the athletics page and in the Student & Family Handbook: whether girls may also join in spring is NOT resolved.
- Boys' Flag Football (Fall) and Girls' and Boys' Ultimate Frisbee (Fall and Spring) appear only on the Upper School PE page as interscholastic offerings; the athletics page, the handbooks and the athletics calendar do not list them as teams (the handbook and the July 21, 2026 Nexus post list ultimate frisbee as a before/after-school PE class option, and Flag Football exists as an Upper School club and as a Middle School team). Best reading: no interscholastic team has been confirmed; ask athletics@nuevaschool.org.
- Swimming & Diving and Track & Field (RESOLVED): the athletics page and PE page list separate girls' and boys' teams; the handbooks say "Coed". Treat as one program with girls' and boys' teams.
- Leagues: the athletics handbook says Nueva is a full PSAL member for cross-country, volleyball, basketball, soccer and track and a supplemental WBAL member for tennis, golf and swimming; the Student & Family Handbook puts track and field in WBAL. Not resolved (the athletics handbook is the Upper School's own document and is used first).
- The athletics page says Nueva has "10 different sports in three seasons" in the Upper School but its list names nine (cross-country, volleyball, beach volleyball, tennis, basketball, soccer, golf, swimming & diving, track & field): the count is not explained.
- The Upper School Athletics Handbook says Nueva "currently sponsors teams in cross-country, volleyball, tennis, basketball, soccer, track, swimming, and golf" (the school's own summary of its league sports); its venue table has no fall golf and no JV information.

## Does Nueva have football, hockey, baseball, softball, lacrosse, rugby, wrestling, badminton or water polo? (sports with NO team: not in any published team list, checked Nov 2026)
As of 2026-11-12 (checked against the athletics page "Sports Offered" list, which names every Middle and Upper School team, and every other athletics list in the school's documents). Applies to: 2026-27.
No. None of the school's published team lists (athletics page, Upper School PE page, Student & Family Handbook, Upper School Athletics Handbook, Middle School and Lower School handbook sections, Nexus resource boards) names tackle football, ice hockey, field hockey, baseball, softball, lacrosse, rugby, wrestling, or water polo as a Nueva team. (MaxPreps shows a "Girls Softball" page for Nueva; it is a placeholder: the school lists no softball team.) What Nueva does offer: football-style play only as flag football (Middle School coed flag football in the fall; "Boys' Flag Football" appears on the 2026-27 US PE page without any games, plus a Flag Football club). Some of these appear only as activities, never as teams: Middle School physical education classes include street hockey, softball, badminton, table tennis, pickle ball and climbing, and after-school Upper School PE options include badminton (yoga, dance, weight training and self-defense are the others). A new sport can be proposed to the athletics department (the handbook's "Starting a New Sport" process starts with a student club).

## Athletic leagues, venues and school colors (Upper School)
Source: Upper School Athletics Handbook (Google Doc captured 2026-10-22). Applies to: 2026-27.
- Nueva is in the Central Coast Section (CCS) of the California Interscholastic Federation (CIF). Full member of the Private School Athletic League (PSAL) for cross-country, volleyball, basketball, soccer and track; supplemental member of the West Bay Athletic League (WBAL) for tennis, golf and swimming. Varsity teams can qualify for CCS Championships.
- Home venues: Cross Country: Hallmark Park and the Nueva Hillsborough campus. Girls' Tennis: Cañada College. Girls' Volleyball, Girls' and Boys' Basketball, Boys' Volleyball: Nueva US Gym (San Mateo). Girls' and Boys' Soccer and Boys' Tennis: Skyline College. Girls' Beach Volleyball: Notre Dame Belmont. Golf: Crystal Springs Golf Course (practice at Stanford Golf Course). Swim & Dive: various Bay Area locations (practice at Bay Club). Track & Field: various Bay Area locations (practice at College of San Mateo and Skyline College).
- School colors: Royal Blue and White. Mascot: the Mavericks.

## Upper School PE graduation requirement (how to meet it)
Source: "US P.E. requirement and PE information" page (captured 2026-10-22) and the Nexus post "PE Requirements for Graduation" (published July 21, 2026). Applies to: 2026-27. Note: older Nexus text still on the resource board (2023-24 dates, "two out of three seasons") is out of date.
Each year Upper School students must do ONE of: play one season of a Nueva interscholastic team; or take a before/after-school PE class for a semester (2026-27 offerings: yoga, dance, weight lifting, self-defense; the Nexus post also lists ultimate frisbee and badminton); or earn outside-activity credit (a supervised activity of at least 2 hours a week, signed off by an adult who is not a parent, with the Outside Activity form returned to the Athletic Director). Required for all four years of high school. Outside-activity forms were due Oct 1, 2026 (semester 1) and Mar 1, 2027 (semester 2).

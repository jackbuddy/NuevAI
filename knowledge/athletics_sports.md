# Athletics: every sport and team Nueva lists (canonical list)

Last updated: 2026-11-11

Built by hand from the school's own athletics documents because no single document lists everything and the documents disagree in small ways. Each section says where its facts come from, the date of that source and the school year it applies to. The sources differ on a few sports (golf, ultimate frisbee, flag football): those differences are shown, not hidden. Team schedules (games, practices) are separate, structured records.

## What sports does Nueva offer? All sports and teams at a glance (Upper School, Middle School, Lower School grade 4), by season
Source: US P.E. requirement and PE information page (2026-27 interscholastic offerings) and the Student & Family Handbook athletics sections (updated 1/1/2026). Applies to: 2026-27. Details, levels, venues and source differences are in the records that follow.
- Upper School (grades 9-12) Fall: Girls' Volleyball, Cross-Country (girls and boys), Girls' Tennis, Girls' Golf, Boys' Flag Football, Ultimate Frisbee. Not offered as teams: tackle football, hockey, baseball, softball, lacrosse, rugby, wrestling, water polo.

## Which Upper School sports are listed in only some sources (golf, ultimate frisbee, flag football, swimming): conflicts to know about
As of 2026-11-11, comparing the school's lists for the Upper School. Applies to: 2026-27.
- Core sports that every current list agrees on: Girls' Volleyball, Cross-Country, Girls' Tennis, Boys' and Girls' Basketball, Boys' and Girls' Soccer, Boys' Volleyball, Boys' Tennis, Swimming & Diving, Track & Field, Girls' Beach Volleyball, and golf.
- Golf: the Student & Family Handbook (updated 1/1/2026) says "Coed Golf" in the spring; the Upper School Athletics Handbook venue table lists "Boys Golf" (spring); the Upper School PE page and the July 21, 2026 Nexus post list Girls' Golf in the fall and Boys' Golf in the spring.
- Boys' Flag Football and Girls' and Boys' Ultimate Frisbee appear only on the Upper School PE page (2026-27); the handbook and the Nexus post do not list them. Flag Football also exists as an Upper School student club (Tuesdays after school) and as a Middle School team.
- Swimming & Diving is "Coed" in the handbook and the Nexus post and "Girls' and Boys'" on the PE page.
- The Upper School Athletics Handbook says Nueva "currently sponsors teams in cross-country, volleyball, tennis, basketball, soccer, track, swimming, and golf" (the school's own summary of its league sports). The athletics office has not confirmed one final list: see which sports to confirm with athletics@nuevaschool.org.

## Does Nueva have football, hockey, baseball, softball, lacrosse, rugby, wrestling, badminton or water polo? (sports with NO team: not in any published team list)
As of 2026-11-11 (checked against every athletics list in the school's documents). Applies to: 2026-27.
No. None of the school's published team lists (Upper School PE page, Student & Family Handbook, Upper School Athletics Handbook, Middle School and Lower School handbook sections, Nexus resource boards) names tackle football, ice hockey, field hockey, baseball, softball, lacrosse, rugby, wrestling, or water polo as a Nueva team. What Nueva does offer: football-style play only as flag football (Middle School coed flag football in the fall; Upper School Boys' Flag Football on the 2026-27 PE page, plus a Flag Football club). Some of these appear only as activities, never as teams: Middle School physical education classes include street hockey, softball, badminton, table tennis, pickle ball and climbing, and after-school Upper School PE options include badminton (yoga, dance, weight training and self-defense are the others). A new sport can be proposed to the athletics department (the handbook's "Starting a New Sport" process starts with a student club).

## Middle School and Lower School (grade 4) sports by season, 2026-27
Source: Student & Family Handbook, Middle School "Athletics" section (Nexus capture 2026-09-04; updated 1/1/2026) and Lower School "Athletics" section. Applies to: 2026-27.
- Middle School (grades 5-8; grade 4 may also play): Fall: Coed Flag Football, Coed Cross Country, Coed Swim, Coed Golf. Early Winter: Girls' Soccer, Boys' Basketball. Late Winter: Girls' Basketball, Boys' Soccer. Spring: Girls' Volleyball, Coed Track and Field, Tennis (listed as "Tennis" and "Coed Tennis").
- Teams play over four seasons from fall through spring in the West Bay Athletic League (WBAL). Every student with interest is encouraged to join; sports may have several teams per sport (7th/8th VA and VB, 5th/6th JVA, JVB1, JVB2, 4th-JVC appear on the calendar). Fourth graders may not be allowed to play swimming, golf or tennis. Team sizes in golf, swim and tennis may be limited by facilities. Announcements of confirmed offerings are made before each season.
- Lower School (grades PreK-4) handbook list for grade 4: Fall: Boys' Flag Football, Coed Cross Country, Coed Swim, Coed Golf; Early Winter: Girls' Soccer, Boys' Basketball; Late Winter: Girls' Basketball, Boys' Soccer; Spring: Girls' Volleyball, Coed Track and Field, Coed Tennis; not every offering is open to a Lower School student because of league rules.

## Athletic leagues, venues and school colors (Upper School)
Source: Upper School Athletics Handbook (Google Doc captured 2026-10-22). Applies to: 2026-27.
- Nueva is in the Central Coast Section (CCS) of the California Interscholastic Federation (CIF). Full member of the Private School Athletic League (PSAL) for cross-country, volleyball, basketball, soccer and track; supplemental member of the West Bay Athletic League (WBAL) for tennis, golf and swimming. Varsity teams can qualify for CCS Championships.
- Home venues: Cross Country: Hallmark Park and the Nueva Hillsborough campus. Girls' Tennis: Cañada College. Girls' Volleyball, Girls' and Boys' Basketball, Boys' Volleyball: Nueva US Gym (San Mateo). Girls' and Boys' Soccer and Boys' Tennis: Skyline College. Girls' Beach Volleyball: Notre Dame Belmont. Golf: Crystal Springs Golf Course (practice at Stanford Golf Course). Swim & Dive: various Bay Area locations (practice at Bay Club). Track & Field: various Bay Area locations (practice at College of San Mateo and Skyline College).
- School colors: Royal Blue and White. Mascot: the Mavericks.

## Upper School PE graduation requirement (how to meet it)
Source: "US P.E. requirement and PE information" page (captured 2026-10-22) and the Nexus post "PE Requirements for Graduation" (published July 21, 2026). Applies to: 2026-27. Note: older Nexus text still on the resource board (2023-24 dates, "two out of three seasons") is out of date.
Each year Upper School students must do ONE of: play one season of a Nueva interscholastic team; or take a before/after-school PE class for a semester (2026-27 offerings: yoga, dance, weight lifting, self-defense; the Nexus post also lists ultimate frisbee and badminton); or earn outside-activity credit (a supervised activity of at least 2 hours a week, signed off by an adult who is not a parent, with the Outside Activity form returned to the Athletic Director). Required for all four years of high school. Outside-activity forms were due Oct 1, 2026 (semester 1) and Mar 1, 2027 (semester 2).

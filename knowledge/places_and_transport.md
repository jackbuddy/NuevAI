# Campus facts by campus: divisions, school-day hours, transportation, libraries

Last updated: 2026-11-11

## San Mateo campus vs Hillsborough campus: which grades and facilities are on each
Source: About Our Campuses page (https://www.nuevaschool.org/about/our-campuses, captured 2026-09-04) and Contact Us page. Applies to: 2026-27.
- Hillsborough Campus, 6565 Skyline Boulevard, Hillsborough, CA 94010 (main line 650-350-4600): the Lower School (grades PreK-4) and Middle School (grades 5-8). Shared facilities: two playgrounds, art studios, science labs, a library (PreK-8), a media lab, a gymnasium, an athletics field, the Innovation Lab (I-Lab), a garden and a cafe. The Lower School is in the historic Crocker mansion; the Middle School is in the Hillside Learning Complex (opened 2007, LEED Gold); the Science and Environmental Center opened January 2020. The campus has 33 acres.
- San Mateo Campus, 131 E 28th Avenue, San Mateo, CA 94403 (front desk 650-235-7100): the Upper School (grades 9-12), built on the site of the former Bay Meadows racetrack (opened 2014, LEED Gold). It has the Writing & Research Center (WRC, the library) and an underground parking structure for staff and visitors.
- Nueva has exactly two campuses. Pre-K through 8th grade is in Hillsborough; 9th through 12th grade is in San Mateo.

## School day hours by division: when school starts and ends, drop-off and pick-up
Source: Student & Family Handbook (Nexus capture 2026-09-04, limited updates 1/1/2026): Middle School, Lower School and Upper School "Arrival, Attendance and Absences" sections. Applies to: 2026-27 (special-schedule days differ).
- Upper School (grades 9-12, San Mateo): the school day begins at 8:55 a.m. and ends at 3:10 p.m. (block times are in the Upper School bell schedule).
- Middle School (grades 5-8, Hillsborough): the school day begins at 8:10 a.m. and ends at 3:15 p.m.; classrooms open at 8 a.m.; students arriving after 8:10 check in at the Middle School office. Drop-off begins 7:50 a.m. at the yellow curb on the field side of the Middle School loop. Before-Care runs 7:30 to 8:10 a.m. in the Library (extendedday@nuevaschool.org); Homework Club for Middle School runs after school until 6 p.m.
- Lower School (PreK-4, Hillsborough): drop-off starts 7:55 a.m. (PreK-K on the lower loop road by the PreK entrance; grades 1-4 along the Lower School courtyard walkway). Classroom instruction generally ends at 3:10; the earliest classroom dismissal is 3:15.
- Afternoon pick-up (Hillsborough): Middle-School-only and mixed MS/LS sibling cars arrive 3:00-3:30 p.m.; Lower-School-only cars arrive 3:30-3:45 p.m.

## Transportation to Nueva: school buses, Caltrain Go Pass, BART, carpool and parking
Source: Transportation page (https://www.nuevaschool.org/school-life/transportation-and-directions), read 2026-11-11. Applies to: 2026-27. Questions: bus@nuevaschool.org or 650-350-4600.
- Buses: Nueva runs six routes serving Hillsborough and four serving San Mateo, plus a daily route between the campuses. Hillsborough: two San Francisco routes (east and west sides) and three South routes (Los Altos/Portola Valley/Woodside; Stanford/Menlo Park; Palo Alto/East Palo Alto). San Mateo: one San Francisco route (west side) and one South route (Woodside, western Palo Alto, Menlo Park). Commute under one hour. Funds are available for families receiving tuition financial assistance, and a sibling discount is offered. The front desk at both campuses is staffed until the last drop-off of the day.
- Caltrain: Go Passes are available at no additional cost to Upper School students and to staff based at the San Mateo campus; the station is a two-block walk. A lost Go Pass sticker is not replaced by Caltrain; Nueva buys a new one and tells you the cost. BART has a student discount program (application on the Nexus Transportation resource board).
- Parking: San Mateo faculty, staff and visitors park only in the underground parking structure; students may not drive to campus unless they have express permission from the Dean of Students. Overflow parking for events is at the San Mateo County Event Center lot next door. Hillsborough visitors may park in the upper or lower lot.
- Bus timing: drivers leave on time without exception; be at the stop 5 minutes before departure. Late for afternoon pick-up: call the campus reception desk (San Mateo 650-235-7100 option 0; Hillsborough 650-350-4600 option 0).

## Libraries and research help: Hillsborough Library (PreK-8) and the San Mateo Writing & Research Center (grades 9-12)
Source: library pages on nuevaschool.org (Hillsborough Library and Writing & Research Center, captured 2026-09-04). Applies to: 2026-27. Opening hours are not published on these pages.
- Hillsborough Campus Library serves the Lower and Middle schools (weekly Lower School visits, Lit Club, Book Fair, literary magazine). Staff: Lower and Middle School Librarian (mdelapena@nuevaschool.org), Library Assistant (gdupar@nuevaschool.org), Library and Administrative Assistant (chuth@nuevaschool.org). Before-Care for the Middle School is held in the Library.
- San Mateo Writing & Research Center (WRC): the Upper School library and writing center: fiction and nonfiction collections, 20 academic databases (on and off campus), e-books and audiobooks via OverDrive, one-on-one writing coaching, peer tutoring and a student book club. Staff: Humanities Director and WRC Director (jpaull@nuevaschool.org) and two Upper School librarians (kkintz@nuevaschool.org, mgu@nuevaschool.org). Students book peer tutoring and writing coaching in the WCOnline scheduler linked on the Nexus Upper School resource board.

# NuevAI terms of use

NuevAI is a school information assistant for The Nueva School community, provided as a student/staff project and not as an official school service.

- Answers are generated from school documents, calendars and shared files and can be wrong or out of date. Check important dates and policies with the school.
- Only share files or emails with nuevai.updates@gmail.com that you are allowed to share, and never student-level data such as rosters, grades or contact details.
- The service is provided as is, without warranty, and may change or stop at any time.
- Contact: nuevai.updates@gmail.com

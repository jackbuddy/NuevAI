## Colors
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Deep navy (backgrounds) | #071630 |
| Navy (text on white, panels) | #0B2145 |

| White | #FAFAFA |

## Type
One bold geometric sans throughout (the app uses Manrope; the deck uses Canva's bold sans). Headlines 115-250 px, body 55-67 px, disclaimer 28 px.

## Wordmark
"Nuev" in white (or navy on white) + "AI" in brand blue, ultra-bold, tight tracking. Same on every slide.

## Voice
Dry, relatable student humor. Short. No "AI-powered", no exclamation marks.

## QR
`nuevai-qr.png` encodes https://nuev-ai.vercel.app (error correction H, navy on white, 4-module quiet zone). If the URL changes, regenerate it and replace the image on slide 4.
